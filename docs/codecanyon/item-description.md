# InfinityCod — Cash on Delivery for WooCommerce (Algeria) — CodeCanyon item description

## Short description (listing teaser)

The all-in-one Cash on Delivery plugin for Algeria: a one-page mobile-first
checkout, official 58-wilaya & 1541-commune geography, carrier integrations
(Yalidine, ZR Express, Maystro, Noest, E-COM, DHD), WhatsApp automation,
anti-fraud Shield, quantity offers and P&L analytics. Online payment included
(CIB/Edahabia via Chargily, Visa/Mastercard via Stripe, PayPal).

## Long description

**Turn visitors into delivered orders.**

InfinityCod replaces the heavy WooCommerce checkout with a lightning-fast,
mobile-first order form built for the Algerian market: the customer picks the
wilaya and commune, chooses home or stopdesk delivery, sees the exact price
live, and confirms in seconds. The order lands in your dashboard as a real
WooCommerce order — no cart, no friction, no lost sales.

### Everything included — full edition, no upsells

- **One-page COD checkout** — shortcode, Gutenberg block and Elementor
  widget. Live price, quantity offers, free-shipping threshold, urgency
  timer, dark mode, full RTL/Arabic.
- **Official geography** — 58 wilayas and 1541 communes (French + Arabic),
  preloaded in one click. Home & stopdesk rates per wilaya, quantity tiers,
  delivery days shown to the customer.
- **Carrier integrations** — create parcels in one click from the orders
  screen (Yalidine, ZR Express, Maystro, Noest, E-COM, DHD/Ecotrack).
  Tracking numbers sync automatically.
- **WhatsApp automation** — order confirmation link, shipment notice,
  abandoned-cart reminders, daily sales report to your own WhatsApp, and
  post-delivery review requests.
- **Anti-fraud Shield** — honeypot, signed timestamps, rate limits by
  IP/phone/email, local + community blacklist, and an adaptive risk score
  that learns from your own delivery history (returns and never-delivered
  numbers raise the score automatically).
- **Online payment** — CIB/Edahabia via Chargily Pay, Visa/Mastercard via
  Stripe, PayPal. Customers choose COD or online payment on the same form.
- **P&L analytics** — revenue by wilaya, product and carrier; confirmation
  and delivery rates; return provisions; monthly accounting export (CSV).
- **Growth toolkit** — loyalty points with automatic returning-customer
  discount, customer tracking portal, Meta/TikTok/Google pixels with
  anti-leak guard, SEO product JSON-LD, wilaya landing pages, PWA merchant
  app with sound + browser notifications for new orders.
- **Signed updates** — hourly check, Ed25519-signed manifests, SHA-256
  verified packages, automatic backup before every update, one-click
  rollback. Optional email/WhatsApp alerts when a new version ships.

### Why merchants choose InfinityCod

- Built for Algeria first: language, geography, carriers, payment rails.
- Loads fast on 3G: vanilla JS, no jQuery, CSS under 15 KB, conditional
  assets only on pages with the form.
- Your data stays on your site. No account required, no phone-home for
  licenses — the only network calls are the integrations you configure.

### Requirements

WordPress 6.0+, WooCommerce 6.0+, PHP 7.4+. Works with Astra, OceanWP,
GeneratePress, Flatsome, Divi, WoodMart and classic themes.

### Support

6 months of support included with your purchase. Documentation inside the
package (`documentation/documentation.md`) — installation takes about five
minutes and the setup wizard guides you through wilayas, rates and carriers.
